# 🚀 Core Features

This page details the functional modules of the NeuroPilot application.

---

## 1. Task Management & Quick Capture
Designed to reduce the barrier to entry for logging tasks, preventing "task paralysis."

* **Quick Capture**: A prominent input on the home screen to quickly add tasks without filling out complex forms.
* **Priority System**: High, Medium, and Low priorities with distinct visual color coding.
* **Sub-tasks**: Ability to break down large tasks into smaller, less overwhelming steps (critical for ADHD).
* **XP Rewards**: Completing tasks awards XP based on the priority level.

---

## 2. Habit Tracking
Focuses on consistency rather than perfection.

* **Streak System**: Tracks consecutive days of completion.
* **Categories**: Health, Focus, Movement, Mindfulness, Sleep, Social, Routine.
* **Flexible Frequency**: Daily, Weekdays, Weekends, or Custom days.
* **Reminder Times**: Specific time alerts for time-sensitive habits (e.g., medication).

---

## 3. Focus Mode & Pomodoro
A gamified timer system to help users initiate and maintain focus.

* **Custom Presets**: 
    * *Deep Work* (50m focus, 10m break)
    * *Pomodoro* (25m focus, 5m break)
    * *Short Burst* (15m focus, 3m break)
* **Gamified Progress**: Earn XP for every minute spent focusing.
* **ADHD Tips**: Contextual tips shown during different phases to guide behavior.

---

## 4. Focus Shield 🛡️
A unique feature to protect the user's attention during Focus Sessions.

* **Notification Suppression**: Cancels all pending app notifications when active.
* **DND Integration**: Provides deep links to system Do Not Disturb settings to block external distractions (calls, texts).
* **Auto-Deactivate**: Automatically restores notification schedules when the focus session is completed or abandoned.

---

## 5. Gamification & Progression
The engine that drives engagement.

* **XP & Levels**: Continuous progression system.
* **Achievements**: Badge unlocks for milestones (e.g., "First 100 Focus Minutes").
* **Streaks**: Visual reinforcement of daily app usage.
