# 🌟 Product Overview: NeuroPilot

## 🎯 Vision & Purpose

NeuroPilot is a mobile application designed to help individuals with ADHD and neurodivergence navigate their daily tasks, build sustainable habits, and achieve deep focus.

Traditional productivity apps often fail neurodivergent users by being either too complex (causing overwhelm) or too simple (causing loss of interest). NeuroPilot solves this by combining:

1. **Low Friction**: Quick capture and simple interfaces.
2. **Gamification**: XP, levels, and badges to provide the dopamine hits needed for motivation.
3. **Adaptive UI**: Calming colors and reduced motion options to prevent sensory overload.

---

## 👥 Target Audience

- **Primary**: Adults and children with ADHD or executive dysfunction.
- **Secondary**: Anyone looking for a highly visual, gamified, and low-friction productivity tool.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Aesthetics

NeuroPilot uses a premium, high-contrast visual design that feels alive and responsive.

### Dynamic Time-of-Day Theming

To support natural circadian rhythms and prevent screen fatigue, the app automatically transitions between 4 custom themes based on the time of day:

| Time                 | Theme     | Palette Feel                                                 |
| -------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🌅 **06:00 – 11:59** | **Dawn**  | Warm amber & rose sunrise, soft cream backgrounds            |
| ☀️ **12:00 – 17:59** | **Day**   | Clean whites, deep purple & cyan accents                     |
| 🌆 **18:00 – 20:59** | **Dusk**  | Deep twilight navy, soft violet & warm amber                 |
| 🌙 **21:00 – 05:59** | **Night** | OLED black (now updated to Deep Navy/Teal), electric accents |

### Visual Principles

- **Glow Effects**: Subtle shadows with color matching the component's accent.
- **Haptics**: Tactile feedback for every major interaction to ground the user.
- **Micro-animations**: Spring physics for buttons and cards.
